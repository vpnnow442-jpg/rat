Privacy

What is stored

  • Your profile name and colour, your runs, your goal, your mode and colour settings.

Where it is stored

  • Without an account, only in this browser on this device (browser local storage). Nothing leaves your device.
  • If accounts are switched on and you create one, your email, username and leaderboard scores are stored with our sign-in provider (Supabase). Your username and best scores are shown publicly on the leaderboards. Your email is not shown.
  • No ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking scripts are used at the moment. If that changes, this notice will be updated and you will be asked first.

What is not protected

  • Anyone who uses this browser can see this data. It is not encrypted and is not behind a password.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the page, may keep standard visit logs (like IP address) as any web host does.
  • Clearing your browser data erases everything, so keep a JSON backup if you want to keep your history.

Your choices

  • Export a backup, delete your history, or delete everything at any time using the controls below.
