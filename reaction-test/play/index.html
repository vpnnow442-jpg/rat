Reaction tests

Simple Reaction Time TestWait for the screen to change colour, then click as fast as you can. This is the classic reaction time test.Audio Reaction Time TestNo colours and no words. Listen for the beep, then click as soon as you hear it. A pure sound reaction test.Target Click Reaction TestWait for a target to appear in a random spot, then click it as fast as you can. Clicking empty space counts as a mistake.Arrow Choice Reaction TestAn arrow appears. Press that direction on your keyboard, or tap that side of the box. Wrong directions count as mistakes.
