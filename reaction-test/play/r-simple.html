Simple Reaction Time Test

Wait for the screen to change colour, then click as fast as you can. This is the classic reaction time test.

Start the test

What the research says

  • In a lab study of 1,469 adults aged 18 to 65, the average simple visual reaction time was about 231 ms, or about 213 ms after correcting for equipment delay. It got slower by about 0.55 ms for each year of age.
  • Online tests usually read higher than lab tests, because your screen, mouse and browser each add a little delay. One reaction time site reports a median of about 270 ms.

Sources

  • Woods et al. 2015, Frontiers in Human Neuroscience
  • Reaction time test benchmark article

Results in a browser include a little delay from your device, so compare yourself with your own past scores more than with other people's.
