Ranks

Your score is measured in tiles. Higher scores are better. Ranks run from Bronze up to Champion.

RankScore neededHow rare (rough guide)
Bronze4 tiles or moreMost players reach this
Silver7 tiles or moreRoughly top 30%
Gold10 tiles or moreRoughly top 10%
Platinum13 tiles or moreRoughly top 3%
Diamond16 tiles or moreRoughly top 1%
Champion20 tiles or moreVery few reach this

The ranks and rarity labels are rough guides set by us. They are not measured norms from a study of players.
