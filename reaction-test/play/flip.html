Ranks

Your score is measured in correct of 12. Higher scores are better. Ranks run from Bronze up to Champion.

RankScore neededHow rare (rough guide)
Bronze4 correct of 12 or moreMost players reach this
Silver6 correct of 12 or moreRoughly top 30%
Gold8 correct of 12 or moreRoughly top 10%
Platinum9 correct of 12 or moreRoughly top 3%
Diamond11 correct of 12 or moreRoughly top 1%
Champion12 correct of 12 or moreVery few reach this

The ranks and rarity labels are rough guides set by us. They are not measured norms from a study of players.
