Ranks

Your score is measured in hits of 20. Higher scores are better. Ranks run from Bronze up to Champion.

RankScore neededHow rare (rough guide)
Bronze6 hits of 20 or moreMost players reach this
Silver9 hits of 20 or moreRoughly top 30%
Gold12 hits of 20 or moreRoughly top 10%
Platinum15 hits of 20 or moreRoughly top 3%
Diamond18 hits of 20 or moreRoughly top 1%
Champion20 hits of 20 or moreVery few reach this

The ranks and rarity labels are rough guides set by us. They are not measured norms from a study of players.
