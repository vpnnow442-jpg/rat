Ranks

Your score is measured in blocks dodged. Higher scores are better. Ranks run from Bronze up to Champion.

RankScore neededHow rare (rough guide)
Bronze5 blocks dodged or moreMost players reach this
Silver10 blocks dodged or moreRoughly top 30%
Gold15 blocks dodged or moreRoughly top 10%
Platinum20 blocks dodged or moreRoughly top 3%
Diamond30 blocks dodged or moreRoughly top 1%
Champion40 blocks dodged or moreVery few reach this

The ranks and rarity labels are rough guides set by us. They are not measured norms from a study of players.
