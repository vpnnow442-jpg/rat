Ranks

Your score is measured in correct of 6. Higher scores are better. Ranks run from Bronze up to Champion.

RankScore neededHow rare (rough guide)
Bronze2 correct of 6 or moreMost players reach this
Silver3 correct of 6 or moreRoughly top 30%
Gold4 correct of 6 or moreRoughly top 10%
Platinum5 correct of 6 or moreRoughly top 3%
Diamond6 correct of 6 or moreRoughly top 1%
Champion6 correct of 6 or moreVery few reach this

The ranks and rarity labels are rough guides set by us. They are not measured norms from a study of players.
