Ranks

Your score is measured in % on target. Higher scores are better. Ranks run from Bronze up to Champion.

RankScore neededHow rare (rough guide)
Bronze30 % on target or moreMost players reach this
Silver45 % on target or moreRoughly top 30%
Gold60 % on target or moreRoughly top 10%
Platinum75 % on target or moreRoughly top 3%
Diamond90 % on target or moreRoughly top 1%
Champion95 % on target or moreVery few reach this

The ranks and rarity labels are rough guides set by us. They are not measured norms from a study of players.
