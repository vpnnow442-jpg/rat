Ranks

Your score is measured in digits. Higher scores are better. Ranks run from Bronze up to Champion.

RankScore neededHow rare (rough guide)
Bronze4 digits or moreMost players reach this
Silver6 digits or moreRoughly top 30%
Gold8 digits or moreRoughly top 10%
Platinum10 digits or moreRoughly top 3%
Diamond12 digits or moreRoughly top 1%
Champion14 digits or moreVery few reach this

The ranks and rarity labels are rough guides set by us. They are not measured norms from a study of players.
